# 🤟 SignX

### AI-Powered Indian Sign Language Accessibility Platform

> **Breaking communication barriers through AI, Computer Vision, NLP, and 3D Sign Language.**

SignX is an accessibility platform designed for deaf and hard-of-hearing users. It combines Indian Sign Language (ISL), computer vision, natural language processing, and 3D avatar technology to provide accessible communication and digital content.

The platform supports both **Sign-to-Text** and **Text-to-Sign** workflows, allowing users to communicate through sign language while also receiving information through a 3D ISL avatar.
